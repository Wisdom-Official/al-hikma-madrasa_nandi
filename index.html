About Our Madrasa

Dedicated to Moral & Spiritual Excellence

Welcome to Nandi Al-Hikma Holiday Madrasa

Nandi Al-Hikma Holiday Madrasa provides structured, high-quality Islamic learning tailored specifically for students during school vacation periods. Our specialized curriculum ensures students remain connected to their faith, Quranic recitation, Islamic history, and jurisprudence.

With structured classes from Standard 1 through Standard 8, our team of dedicated teachers guides young learners through standard educational benchmarks set forth by the Wisdom Education Board.

Our Vision

To raise a generation of morally grounded, knowledgeable, and compassionate Muslim youths who exemplify Islamic values in their personal and social lives.

Our Mission

To deliver comprehensive holiday religious education with modern teaching methodologies, empowering students with Quranic comprehension, Hadith studies, and practical Islamic living.
